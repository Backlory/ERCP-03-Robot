## Device-boundary interpretation

The connected PLC controls the robot body: endoscope motion, lifter, and
water/gas/suction. `MAIN_ERCP` belongs to a separate, currently disconnected
ERCP cart that controls the sphincterotome, injectors, guide wire, and force
feedback. Absence of the ERCP-cart symbols on this robot PLC is expected and is
not a robot-interface failure.

The gold document should therefore be split into at least two device profiles:

1. Robot-body PLC (`MAIN.*` and `MAIN.Info_Feedback_ToMaster.*`).
2. ERCP-cart PLC (`MAIN_ERCP.*` and `POU_Ercp_CycleExecute.*`).

`MAIN.ERCP_Online_flag` is a robot-side integration field and should be listed
as conditional on the cart integration PLC project.

RobotSystem was corrected to detect the representative
`MAIN_ERCP.ERCP_Info_Feedback_ToMaster` symbol read-only when ADS connects.
If it is absent, cart polling is skipped automatically and the cart groups are
reported unavailable without degrading the robot-body ADS connection.
